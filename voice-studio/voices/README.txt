Each prepared voice looks like:

  voices/mira-default/
    ref.wav      6-12 s, single clean take (gitignored)
    ref.txt      exact words spoken in ref.wav
    config.json  exaggeration / cfg / sample rate
    clips/       4-12 s cleaned pieces (gitignored)

One reference per mood. Do not mix three MP3s into one ref.wav.
