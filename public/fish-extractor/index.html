FISH EXTRACTOR

A heist, except the vault is a pond and the cops are feral.

Hire a crew. Arm them. Drag-select. Right-click to move. Build a counter, drop a register on it, and the peaceful ones will line up to buy.

BRIEFING

Left-drag a box to select several. Left-click empty ground to drop the group. Right-click to move. Click a biting unit to set the hook.

Build opens walls, counters, and registers. A register only sits on a finished counter. Passives queue at registers; if there is no register they crowd your crew at arm’s length and your bullets will drop them — they also soak shots meant for savages.

Job 1 is a trickle. Don’t wipe the customers. Fish stay on the person who caught them — five each unless you bought bags. Heavier bags, slower walk. Pond and LZ swap corners each job — never the same corner. Later jobs often wrap the pond in an electric fence — HACK the terminal (captcha). Tanks cannot fit the gate; the helicopter flies over. Hire from three faces a job. Units take a cut of every sale, level like a grinding RPG, and get ugly if you touch their cut. Click the cash box for CDs and bonds. The amphibious tank fishes the dark hole.
