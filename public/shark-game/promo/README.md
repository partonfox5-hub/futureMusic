# Shark Game promotional assets

Shark Game is a single-player Meta Quest underwater survival game by FutureMusicOnline. Pilot a small submarine through dark ocean water, track a colossal shark, and fight it with torpedoes. Attacks crack the windshield, reduce propulsion, and cause lighting failures. Leave the submarine with a flashlight and use an underwater welding torch to repair it. A replayable introduction teaches piloting, combat, swimming, and repair. The ocean contains fish, jellyfish, kelp, coral, and large rock formations.

## Artwork direction for Grok

Use the exact title **SHARK GAME**. Keep the cinematic dark teal ocean, enormous grey shark with an open jaw, compact black bubble-canopy submarine with bright headlights, and towering coral/rock formations. Keep all lettering and the shark's essential facial features comfortably inside Meta's safe area for every output size. Do not add a price, platform badges, taglines, rating symbols, or extra promotional text to the store covers. Preserve strong white title contrast and readability at thumbnail size.

The cover artwork and icon were created using the built-in image-generation tool. The cover exports use centered crops of the corrected master; Meta's universal-cover tool generated the versions saved in the listing. The hero image was separately recomposed to move its title inward and checked in Meta's safe-area preview. These are promotional illustrations, not gameplay screenshots.

## Reproduction briefs

Built-in image-generation mode was used, without a CLI/API fallback. The final design briefs were:

**Cover master:** Cinematic promotional illustration for Shark Game, based on the actual game's grey giant shark, black submarine with bubble canopy and headlights, dark teal water, jellyfish, fish, and towering coral/rock spires. Use only the exact stacked white title SHARK / GAME in a compact central region so landscape, square, and portrait crops preserve the complete lettering. No badges, prices, other text, blood, or gore.

**Safe hero revision:** Preserve the same shark, submarine, ocean lighting, scenery, and white stacked SHARK / GAME lettering. Recompose as a wide hero banner, moving the title inward to roughly the 25–47% horizontal region and keeping it around the vertical center. Keep the shark face and submarine within the central composition; let only scenery and light beams extend into the outer bleed area. Export to 3000 x 900 RGB PNG.

These briefs record the final design intent; they are not a verbatim transcript of every generation prompt.
