# Shark Game Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026.

Future Music Collective (Fox Parton), also publishing as FutureMusicOnline, provides **Shark Game**, the single-player VR submarine survival game for Meta Quest (Android package `com.futuremusic.shark`). This policy explains the game's data practices and how to contact us about privacy or deletion.

## Information used by the game

- Headset and controller tracking input is used on the device to render your viewpoint and operate the game. We do not receive recordings of this input.
- The app saves a local tutorial-completion file so it can remember whether you have completed the introduction. This file remains in the app's own storage on your headset. We do not receive or use it for advertising.
- The game does not ask you to provide your name, email address, contacts, location, camera recordings, or microphone recordings to us. Its game code does not send personal information to our website.
- The Quest app does not require a Future Music Collective account and does not include in-app purchases, advertisements, chat, or user-generated content.
- Meta handles the store purchase, your Meta account, and platform telemetry under Meta's own terms and privacy policy. We cannot delete Meta's records.

## Support and contact messages

If you contact us for support or about privacy, we receive the contact details and message you choose to send. We use these to answer your request and follow up. Contact messages are kept long enough to reply and follow up, except information we must retain for legal, security, or accounting reasons. We do not sell your personal information or use Shark Game's local tutorial file for advertising.

If you use our website, its separate [website privacy policy](https://futuremusic.online/privacy) describes website accounts, contact-form messages, cookies, hosting logs, website purchases, and advertising on some website pages. Those website services are not required to play Shark Game. Service providers used for hosting and email delivery may process information needed to deliver those services.

## Deleting data and contacting us

You can remove the locally stored tutorial-completion data by clearing Shark Game's app data on the headset or uninstalling the app.

To request deletion of information you have sent us:

1. Open our [contact form](https://futuremusic.online/contact).
2. Include the email you used with us, the app name **Shark Game**, and the words **delete my data** or **privacy deletion request**.
3. We will confirm and delete personal information we control, except information we must retain for legal, security, or accounting reasons.

Horizon Store purchase history and Meta account information are controlled by Meta. Requests concerning those records must be made to Meta.

## Children

Shark Game is intended for teens and adults aged 13 or older, subject to the applicable regional age rating. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, use the contact and deletion steps above so we can delete it.

## Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do. This policy is maintained by the Shark Game developer. Questions about privacy, collection, or deletion can be sent through [Future Music Collective's contact form](https://futuremusic.online/contact).
