THE HORDE

Infinite hills under a day and a shorter night — after sunset it goes truly dark, so bring a flashlight. One pistol. Doubling waves to 100, then the fight goes on at peak difficulty. From wave 5, half the horde waits and drips in over a minute. Every five waves the ground shifts to another world in the solar system. Meteors streak the sky. X opens the armory — drones, a WWII stone turret you can double-jump onto, forcefield orbs, a terraform gun, a cyber tank, an attack heli with missiles and a search beam, ripple ray, zero-point gun, mini nuke, a dirt bike, and a laser noodle if you can afford them. B cycles every gun you own. After wave ten, some of the horde start throwing, and leapers start jumping. Strong wings later on buzz like insects. Centipedes slither. Spiders scuttle. Limbs rise all at once.
