Orbital strategy

Planetry

A young galaxy. Worlds cool from magma in thirty seconds. You do not forge them — you claim them. Scout the void, land settlers, raise metal cities, and fire across the dark.

  • Start with 500 materials — scout 100, settler 250.
  • Y opens the strategy cards. Point and press A to spawn.
  • Hold left trigger to multi-select. Right trigger commands a move.
  • Glow rings mark lower atmosphere. Fly in to walk the crust — the galaxy sky sleeps. X returns you to the map.
  • Press A on your flag or city to open the spawn cards. Surface trees, beasts, and buildings are half-size; from space they are pegs.
