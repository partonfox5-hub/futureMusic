SHARK

You are sealed in a pressure cell in a sea with no surface. A giant lives in the walls. Thirty hits kill it. Every third hit it quickens. Whales and krakens bleed chunks that sink. Krakens will wrap the hull and hold you.

Patch the hull from outside after each strike. Steal oxygen from stray tanks — they last longer now. Drive the giant off with torpedoes. If it takes you in the open, you are gone. If you slay it, the cell is yours.

F hatch. R radio in the sub. Outside, hold R near the hull to patch. Desktop: WASD, Shift down, Tab up, mouse, click. Grab fuel cans and spare torpedoes off the floor. VR: grip the wheel, lift or press for depth, push it to thrust, trigger or the red button to fire.
