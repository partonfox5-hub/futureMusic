SHARK

You are sealed in a pressure cell in a sea with no surface. A giant lives in the walls. It may be stalking, hunting, or just passing. Krakens drift and wrap the hull if you swim into their arms. Whales pass in the dark.

Patch the hull from outside after each strike. Steal oxygen from stray tanks. Drive the shark off with torpedoes — three hits in a minute and it flees. Outside, you are slow. If it takes you in the open, you are gone.

F hatch. R radio in the sub. Outside, hold R near the hull to patch. Desktop: WASD, Shift down, Tab up, mouse, click. Grab fuel cans and spare torpedoes off the floor.

Quest 3: tap ENTER VR. Hold a trigger on the wheel to take the helm — turning your hands does nothing until you grip. Left stick X steers, Y is depth (up/down). Right stick is throttle. Poke the red button to fire. Yellow lever is the hatch. Outside, hold trigger near the hull to weld.
