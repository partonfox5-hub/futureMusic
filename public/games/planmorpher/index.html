Planmorpher

Worlds now seed as continents, pangea, archipelagos, mountains, deserts, and ice — each with its own sea. Select nothing, open a planet, and raise or lower the water. Descend into the sky; press X to leave it.

  • Select on the wrist gems: no power. Point at a world and press A (or click) for its menu and sea slider.
  • Form: Fearsome, Divine, or Inspiring. Mortals flee, flock, or take heart for sixty heartbeats.
  • Fly close to a world — a blue sky takes over and you are in its lower atmosphere. Fly the crust. X hurls you back to the map. Uplift raises land through the water shell.
  • Life opens the spore studio — snap Horde-style limbs onto a torso, save ten species, each with its own civ score.
  • Lightning actually strikes. Laser carves riverbeds and burns ships. Scout and settler fly as in Planetry; settlers found cities for the selected spore.
