Worlds seed as continents, pangea, archipelagos, mountains, deserts, and ice — each with its own sea. Open a planet, raise or lower the water, and descend into its sky. Press X to leave it.

  • Select on the wrist gems: no power. Point at a world and press A (or click) for its menu and sea slider.
  • Weather: clouds, thunderstorm, snow, hail. Hold A or trigger to grow clouds in the atmosphere.
  • Fly into a world's atmosphere — triangular markers become the creatures you designed, with full animated limbs. X returns you to the galaxy.
  • Life opens the maker. Trigger a body part to swap it. Spend 100 life points on stats. Grove paints forests and berry bushes on land.
