Planmorpher

You are a spirit in the depth of space. Hold a world into being. Watch it cool, take rain, and raise land. Then tend it — storm, lightning, life, stone, and fire from the sky.

  • Hold trigger / click to grow a molten sphere. Longer hold, larger world.
  • Left stick or WASD to drift. Right stick / mouse to look. Q/E or stick up-down for height.
  • Point at a labeled wrist gem to pick a power. Life opens the bestiary.
  • Meteors crater worlds. Trees and creatures rise from the crust. Land walks, water swims.
  • Quest 3: tap Enter VR. Desktop works in the void as well.
