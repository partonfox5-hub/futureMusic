You are sealed in a pressure cell in a sea with no surface. A giant lives in the walls. It may be hunting. Whales pass in the dark. Sonar cannot tell them apart.

Patch the hull from outside after each strike. Steal oxygen from stray tanks. Drive it off with torpedoes when it lunges. Outside, you are slow. If it takes you in the open, you are gone.

F hatch. R radio in the sub. Outside, hold R near the hull to patch. Desktop: WASD, Shift down, Tab up, mouse, click. Grab fuel cans and spare torpedoes off the floor.

Quest 3 (browser): tap ENTER VR at the bottom. Grip the wheel to steer and change depth. Left and right thumbsticks together run port and starboard thrust — both sticks forward to go the way the nose points, both back to reverse. Push the red button to fire. Yellow lever is the hatch. Outside, hold trigger near the hull; a welding torch appears in your hands and throws bubbles as you patch.
