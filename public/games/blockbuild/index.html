A quiet infinite workshop

Blockbuild

Fly an empty sky. Snap studded bricks on a level table. They interlock when they kiss. Wrist palette: outer ring is color, inner ring is size. Center tools change kind, gravity, zoom, and handedness.

  • Trigger places the ghost brick (or a held one). Snap-to-studs when close.
  • Grip picks up stray bricks. Trigger again to lock them in.
  • A spills ten random pieces. B rotates. Y fires a cannonball that breaks joints.
  • Hold X and the build hand becomes a flame — melt bricks you hold it near.
  • Zoom until you are a giant or a speck. Toggle gravity. Swap the palette wrist for left-handed play.
  • Fig studio on the palette: three carousels for head, torso, and legs. Place copies — they wander and climb stairs you build.

Desktop: WASD fly, mouse look, click place, C/V/B cycle color/size/kind, R rotate, P pile, F grab, Y cannon, X melt, G gravity, [ ] zoom, H swap, M fig studio.
