A quiet infinite workshop

Blockbuild

Fly an empty sky. Snap studded bricks on a level table. They interlock when they kiss. No wrist palette — A, B, and the right stick-click run the kit. Left stick-click opens a 3D workshop menu to save and load maps.

  • Trigger places the ghost brick or fig. Snap-to-studs when close.
  • A cycles colors. B cycles piece types. Right stick-click toggles brick placing, minifig placing, and empty-handed.
  • Empty-handed: grab existing bricks to move them, pick up figs and toss them.
  • Left stick-click opens the VR menu: save/load three map slots, dump a pile, new table, gravity.
  • Hold X to melt. Tap X to rotate. Y fires a cannonball that breaks joints. [ ] zooms the table.

Desktop: WASD fly, mouse look, click place, Tab mode, C/B color/kind, R rotate, Esc menu, 1–3 save, 4–6 load, P pile, F grab, Y cannon, X melt, G gravity.
