How to Play

  1. Build / Repair — Place Tetris-like wall pieces (R rotate, click place). Each wall segment costs 1 pt. Use Vote: End Build Early to skip the timer (both players in online). Fully enclose at least one keep (orthogonally — diagonals don’t count!). Between rounds you build again with your treasury.
  2. Round bonus — Each round grants 20 × round number points to both players (R1: 20, R2: 40, …).
  3. Battle — Cannons auto-deploy; click map to fire (full range, low arc). Archers auto-target units. AI fires back. Units cross river at 25% speed; place bridges on water. Flags: +pts label, 3s capture in range. F FPS.
  4. Loser wheel — Only if you lost more cannons that round and ended with fewer standing than your opponent: spin for a consolation prize.
  5. Between rounds — Spend points building walls, bridges, and structures again (no separate market). Flags pay best.
  6. Structures & damage persist between rounds. Highest score after 6 rounds wins — or eliminate the foe’s last keep.

Hotkeys: R rotate · Space skip piece · G toggle gate · F FPS · Esc exit FPS · H help
