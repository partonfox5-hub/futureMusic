About this pass

Stand still to inspect skin and contact response. Shape controls stay within a conservative range for this rig. Motion and collisions are approximations using bones and capsules; the model has no recorded animation clips or volumetric tissue mesh.

Existing 2K color maps contain synthetic fills and projected portraits. Mapping, normals and roughness are corrected; new scan detail is not claimed. UVs missing at welded material seams are locally reconstructed.

Quest starts at 72 Hz, with antialiasing and moderate foveation. Measure on your headset; choose Faster if frames drop. Texture compression and authored motion capture are deferred. Graphical browser and headset performance were not verified in this pass.
